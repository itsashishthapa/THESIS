\multicolumn{3}{@{}l}{\textbf{Time, energy, and economic quantities}} \\*
$t,i,j,\ell$ & Time, summation, and component indices; the applicable meaning and range are stated with each equation & -- \\
$N$ & Number of decision steps in the scheduling horizon & -- \\
$\Delta t_{\mathrm h},\Delta t_{\mathrm s}$ & Duration of one time step in hours and seconds, respectively; $\Delta t_{\mathrm s}=3600\Delta t_{\mathrm h}$ & h; s \\
$S_t$ & Spot electricity price & EUR/MWh \\
$S_t^{\mathrm{buy}},S_t^{\mathrm{sell}}$ & Electricity purchase and sale prices & EUR/MWh \\
$\Delta_s$ & Fixed difference between the purchase and sale prices & EUR/MWh \\
$P_t^{\mathrm S}$ & Grid-side electrical power; positive for purchase & kW \\
$P_t^+,P_t^-$ & Purchased and sold power magnitudes, respectively & kW \\
$W_t$ & Available wind power & kW \\
$C_t$ & Grid-electricity cost at one time step & EUR \\
$J_{\mathrm{grid}}$ & Total net grid-electricity cost over the scheduling horizon & EUR \\
\multicolumn{3}{@{}l}{\textbf{Battery energy storage}} \\*
$E_{\max}$ & Usable battery energy capacity & kWh \\
$P_{\max}$ & Maximum charging and discharging power & kW \\
$P_{\mathrm{actual},t}$ & Effective storage-side power after efficiency losses & kW \\
$\widehat P_{\mathrm{actual},t}$ & Requested storage-side power before limiting it to the available energy & kW \\
$u_t$ & Normalized battery control command & -- \\
$\mathrm{SOC}_0,\mathrm{SOC}_t$ & Initial battery state of charge and state of charge at the beginning of decision step $t$ & -- \\
$\mathrm{SOC}_N$ & Terminal state of charge after $N$ decisions & -- \\
$\mathrm{SOC}_{\mathrm{term}}$ & Required minimum terminal state of charge & -- \\
$\mathrm{SOC}^{\mathrm{reach}}_t$ & Minimum state of charge that preserves terminal-target reachability & -- \\
$\eta$ & Charging and discharging efficiency & -- \\
$k$ & Steepness parameter of the smooth action and SOC gates & -- \\
$\delta$ & Half-width of the normalized battery-action deadband & -- \\
$\epsilon$ & Power scale for the smooth efficiency transition & kW \\
\multicolumn{3}{@{}l}{\textbf{Cookie production factory and thermal storage}} \\*
$T_{\mathrm{I},t},T_{\mathrm{II},t}$ & HTHX inlet and outlet temperatures, respectively & $^\circ$C \\
$T_{\mathrm{III},t},T_{\mathrm{IV},t}$ & LTHX inlet and outlet temperatures, respectively & $^\circ$C \\
$T_{1,t},T_{4,t}$ & TES outlet temperatures during charging and discharging, respectively & $^\circ$C \\
$T_{2,t},T_{3,t}$ & Steam-generator inlet and outlet temperatures, respectively & $^\circ$C \\
$T_{s,0},T_{s,t},T_{s,N}$ & Initial, beginning-of-step, and terminal TES temperatures & $^\circ$C \\
$\tilde{T}_0$ & Prescribed initial thermal-storage temperature & $^\circ$C \\
$\dot{m}_{\mathrm{I},t},\dot{m}_{\mathrm{II},t}$ & Per-HTHP HTHX inlet and outlet HTF mass-flow rates; equal in the implemented closed loop & kg/s \\
$m_s$ & Mass of the thermal-storage medium & kg \\
$c_{p,\mathrm f}, c_{p,\mathrm s}$ & Specific heat capacities of the heat-transfer fluid and storage medium & kJ/(kg\,K) \\
$R_t$ & Compressor-speed-related control variable & -- \\
$R_{\mathrm{ref}}$ & Reference value used to select the initial compressor-speed root & -- \\
$\beta_{1,t},\beta_{2,t}$ & TES charging-side and discharging-side bypass settings & -- \\
$b_t$ & Signed cookie-factory TES-mode command & -- \\
$\delta_\beta$ & Half-width of the idle band in the signed TES-mode command & -- \\
$\dot{Q}_{s,\mathrm{ch},t}$ & Thermal-storage charging heat rate & kW \\
$\dot{Q}_{s,\mathrm{dch},t}$ & Thermal-storage discharging heat rate & kW \\
$\dot Q_s^{\max}$ & Maximum permitted TES charging or discharging heat rate & kW \\
$P_{\mathrm S}^{\max}$ & Maximum permitted cookie-factory grid power & kW \\
$T_s^{\min},T_s^{\max}$ & Lower and upper TES temperature limits & $^\circ$C \\
$\Delta T_s$ & TES temperature span, $T_s^{\max}-T_s^{\min}$ & $^\circ$C \\
$T_{\mathrm{II,req},t}$ & HTHX outlet temperature required to satisfy the $F_4$ coupling relation & $^\circ$C \\
$\epsilon_{\mathrm{ch}},\epsilon_{\mathrm{dch}}$ & Thermal-storage charging and discharging effectiveness & -- \\
$\gamma$ & Upper bound on the product of charging and discharging heat rates & kW$^2$ \\
$F_{\mathrm{HTHP}}$ & Surrogate model for electrical power demand of one HTHP unit & kW \\
$F_{\mathrm{HTHX}},F_{\mathrm{LTHX}}$ & Surrogate models for high- and low-temperature heat-exchanger outlet temperatures & $^\circ$C \\
$\kappa_{j,t}$ & Coefficient of $R^j$ in the HTHX outlet-temperature surrogate with the other inputs fixed at time $t$; $j=0,\ldots,3$ & $^\circ$C \\
$F_4,F_5$ & Labels for the charging-side coupling and discharging-side mixing relations in Eqs.~\eqref{eq:cookie_charging_coupling} and~\eqref{eq:cookie_discharging_mixing} & n/a \\
\multicolumn{3}{@{}l}{\textbf{Model-based optimization}} \\*
$\xi,\xi^\star$ & Decision vector and optimal decision vector & * \\
$\Omega$ & Domain of the decision variables & n/a \\
$n_\xi,n_h,n_c$ & Numbers of decision variables, equality constraints, and inequality constraints & -- \\
$f(\xi)$ & Objective function & * \\
$h(\xi),c(\xi)$ & Aggregate equality- and inequality-constraint vectors & * \\
$c_j(\xi)$ & $j$th inequality-constraint function & * \\
$s,s_j$ & Slack vector and its $j$th component & * \\
$\mu_{\mathrm b}$ & Logarithmic barrier parameter & * \\
$y,z$ & Equality- and inequality-constraint multipliers & * \\
$J_h,J_c$ & Jacobian matrices of the constraint functions & * \\
$\mathsf{S}=\operatorname{diag}(s)$ & Diagonal matrix of slack variables & * \\
\multicolumn{3}{@{}l}{\textbf{Reinforcement learning and evaluation}} \\*
$E,A$ & State and action spaces of the finite-horizon MDP & n/a \\
$D_t(x)$ & Admissible actions in state $x$ at time step $t$ & n/a \\
$Q_t(\cdot\mid x,a)$ & Transition probability kernel at time step $t$ & -- \\
$p_t(x'\mid x,a)$ & Transition probability density, when it exists & * \\
$g_N(x)$ & Separate terminal-reward function & ** \\
$x,x_t,x'$ & Generic state, state at time step $t$, and next state & * \\
$X_t,A_t$ & Random state and action at time step $t$ & * \\
$a,a_t,a_t^{\mathrm{phys}}$ & Generic action, policy action at time $t$, and corresponding physical setpoints & * \\
$a_{t+1}^{\prime}$ & Next action sampled from the SAC policy for the critic target & * \\
$\mathcal{M}$ & Map from policy actions to physical setpoints & n/a \\
$\pi,\pi_t(\cdot\mid x),\pi_\theta(a\mid x)$ & Policy, time-$t$ conditional action distribution, and parameterized stochastic policy & * \\
$\pi_{\mathrm b}$ & Behavior policy used to collect transitions & n/a \\
$\mu_\theta(x),\mu_{\bar\theta}(x)$ & Deterministic TD3 actor and target actor & * \\
$\theta$ & Actor-parameter vector for TD3 or SAC & * \\
$\phi$ & Generic critic-parameter vector & * \\
$\phi_1,\phi_2$ & TD3 critic-parameter vectors & * \\
$\psi_1,\psi_2$ & SAC critic-parameter vectors & * \\
$\bar\theta,\bar\phi_1,\bar\phi_2$ & TD3 target-network parameter vectors & * \\
$\bar\psi_1,\bar\psi_2$ & SAC target-critic parameter vectors & * \\
$\mathcal{D}$ & Replay buffer; distinct from the admissible-action set $D_t(x)$ & n/a \\
$d_t$ & Episode-end indicator & -- \\
$r_t(x,a),r_t(x_t,a_t),r_t$ & Stage-reward function, realized value, and shorthand for the realized value & ** \\
$r_t^{\mathrm{cookie}}(x_t,a_t)$ & Cookie-factory stage reward & ** \\
$r_t^{\mathrm{reach}}(x_t,a_t)$ & Battery reward including the terminal-SOC reachability penalty & ** \\
$G_t$ & Finite-horizon return from time step $t$ & ** \\
$\gamma_{\mathrm{RL}}$ & Reward discount factor; set to $1$ in all experiments & -- \\
$v_t^\pi,q_t^\pi$ & State-value and action-value functions under policy $\pi$ & ** \\
$v_t^\star,q_t^\star$ & Optimal state-value and action-value functions & ** \\
$\mathcal{A}_t^\pi(x,a)$ & Advantage function, $q_t^\pi(x,a)-v_t^\pi(x)$ & ** \\
$q_\phi$ & Generic parameterized action-value critic & ** \\
$q_{\psi_1},q_{\psi_2}$ & Two parameterized SAC critics & ** \\
$q_{\bar\psi_1},q_{\bar\psi_2}$ & SAC target critics & ** \\
$q_{\phi_1},q_{\phi_2}$ & Two parameterized TD3 critics & ** \\
$q_{\bar\phi_1},q_{\bar\phi_2}$ & TD3 target critics & ** \\
$\widehat v^{\mathrm{tar}}$ & Continuation-value estimate used in a TD target & ** \\
$y_t$ & Generic one-step TD learning target & ** \\
$y_t^{\mathrm{TD3}},y_t^{\mathrm{SAC}}$ & TD3 and SAC critic learning targets & ** \\
$\delta_t^{\mathrm{TD}}$ & Temporal-difference error & ** \\
$\zeta$ & TD3 target-policy smoothing noise & * \\
$L(\phi_j),L(\psi_j)$ & Mean squared TD-error losses of TD3 and SAC critics & (reward units)$^2$ \\
$J_{\mathrm{PG}},J_{\mathrm{TD3}},J_{\mathrm{SAC}}$ & Generic policy-gradient, TD3 actor, and SAC policy objectives & ** \\
$\alpha$ & Weight of the SAC entropy term & ** \\
$\mathcal{H}$ & Policy entropy & -- \\
$\tau$ & Target-network soft-update coefficient & -- \\
$[\,\cdot\,]_+$ & Positive-part operator & same as its argument \\
$v_{\ell,t}$ & Constraint violation in category $\ell$ at time step $t$ & * \\
$v_{\mathrm T,t}$ & Terminal violation, including the final-step indicator; zero before the episode ends & -- (battery); $^\circ$C (cookie factory) \\
$v_{\mathrm{reach},t}$ & Battery terminal-SOC reachability violation & -- \\
$v_{s,t},v_{\mathrm I,t},v_{F_4,t}$ & Cookie-factory TES-temperature, inlet-temperature, and coupling violations & $^\circ$C \\
$v_{Q,t},v_{P,t}$ & Cookie-factory heat-flow and grid-power violations & kW \\
$\lambda_\ell,\lambda_{\mathrm T}$ & Constraint and terminal penalty weights & reward units per violation unit \\
$\lambda_s,\lambda_{\mathrm I},\lambda_Q$ & Cookie-factory TES-temperature, inlet-temperature, and heat-flow penalty coefficients in Eq.~\eqref{eq:cookie_reward} & ** \\
$\lambda_P,\lambda_{F_4},\lambda_{\mathrm{cyc}}$ & Cookie-factory grid-power, coupling, and cyclic-terminal penalty coefficients in Eq.~\eqref{eq:cookie_reward} & ** \\
$\lambda_{\mathrm{SOC}},\lambda_{\mathrm{reach}}$ & Terminal-SOC and reachability penalty weights & ** \\
$R_{\mathrm{ep}}$ & Cumulative undiscounted episode reward & ** \\
$V_{\mathrm{tot},\ell}$ & Sum of recorded violation magnitudes for constraint category $\ell$ & * \\
$f_{\mathrm{viol}}$ & Fraction of time steps with constraint violations & -- \\
$\varepsilon_{\mathrm{viol},\ell}$ & Numerical violation-detection tolerance for constraint category $\ell$ & * \\
$\mathbb{E},\mathbb{E}_\pi,\mathbb{E}_{\mathcal D}$ & Expectation, expectation under policy $\pi$, and expectation over replay-buffer samples & n/a \\
$\mathbf{1}$ & Vector of ones & -- \\
$\mathbb{I},\mathbb{I}_{\{\cdot\}}$ & Indicator function and event-indicator notation & -- \\
