\section*{Nomenclature}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Nomenclature}

The main model and evaluation symbols are listed below. A dash denotes a
dimensionless quantity; ``n/a'' denotes a policy with no single physical
unit. Temperature differences are listed in K and have the same numerical
values in ${}^\circ\mathrm C$. $U_r$ denotes EUR for raw rewards and
returns, or a dimensionless unit for normalized training rewards.

\multicolumn{3}{@{}l}{\textbf{Time, energy, and economic quantities}} \\*

$P_{\max}$ & Battery power scale in the smooth grid-side action map & kW \\

$u_t$ & Normalized battery control command & -- \\

$T_{\mathrm{III},t},T_{\mathrm{IV},t}$ & LTHX inlet and outlet temperatures & $^\circ$C \\
$T_{1,t},T_{4,t}$ & TES outlets during charging and discharging & $^\circ$C \\

$\tilde T_0$ & Prescribed initial TES temperature, equal to $T_{s,0}$ & $^\circ$C \\
$\dot{m}_{\mathrm{I},t},\dot{m}_{\mathrm{II},t}$ & Per-HTHP HTHX inlet and outlet HTF mass-flow rates & kg/s \\

$x_t$ & State vector; implemented network observations are dimensionless & componentwise \\

$\pi$ & Policy for selecting actions & n/a \\

$G_t$ & Return from step $t$ & $U_r$ \\
$v_t^\pi,q_t^\pi$ & State-value and action-value functions & $U_r$ \\
$\gamma_{\mathrm{RL}}$ & Reward discount factor & -- \\
$\alpha$ & SAC entropy weight & $U_r$ \\
$v_{\ell,t}$ & Constraint violation in category $\ell$ & category unit \\
$v_{\mathrm T,t}$ & Terminal SOC or cyclic TES-temperature violation & -- (battery); K (cookie) \\
$v_{\mathrm{reach},t}$ & Terminal-SOC reachability violation & -- \\
$v_{s,t},v_{\mathrm I,t},v_{F_4,t}$ & TES-temperature, inlet-temperature, and coupling violations & K \\
$v_{Q,t},v_{P,t}$ & Heat-flow and grid-power violations & kW \\
$\lambda_\ell$ & General penalty weight per native violation unit & EUR/$[v_{\ell,t}]$ \\
$\lambda_{\mathrm T}$ & General terminal penalty weight & EUR (battery); EUR/K (cookie) \\
$\lambda_s,\lambda_{\mathrm I},\lambda_Q$ & Coefficients for normalized temperature and heat-flow violations & EUR \\
$\lambda_P,\lambda_{F_4},\lambda_{\mathrm{cyc}}$ & Coefficients for normalized grid-power, coupling, and terminal violations & EUR \\
$\lambda_{\mathrm{SOC}},\lambda_{\mathrm{reach}}$ & Battery terminal-SOC and reachability penalty weights & EUR \\
$R_{\mathrm{ep}}$ & Cumulative undiscounted raw episode reward & EUR \\
$V_{\mathrm{tot},\ell}$ & Sum of violation magnitudes in category $\ell$ & $[v_{\ell,t}]$ \\
$f_{\mathrm{viol}}$ & Fraction of time steps with constraint violations & -- \\
$\varepsilon_{\mathrm{viol},\ell}$ & Violation-detection tolerance in category $\ell$ & $[v_{\ell,t}]$ \\
\end{longtable}
\noindent $[v_{\ell,t}]$ denotes the unit of the corresponding violation:
dimensionless for SOC and bypass violations, K for temperature differences,
kW for power and heat rates, and kW$^2$ for a charging--discharging product
residual. Generic vectors have units per component. Learning actions are
dimensionless; the battery environment receives $u_t$, while the cookie
environment receives $(b_t,\dot m_{\mathrm I,t})$ with units (--, kg/s).
Mathematical operators,
optimization auxiliaries, and algorithm-specific network notation are
defined where they appear in the text.
